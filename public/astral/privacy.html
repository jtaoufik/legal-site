Privacy Policy

Last updated September 4, 2026

Astral (the "App") is a PDF, JSON, image, and text utility toolkit published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Quick summary: Astral processes your files and text entirely on your device. Your PDFs, images, and text are never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account and no login. The App uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and shows ads through Google AdMob, which you can remove with a one-time purchase that restores automatically across your devices through your Apple ID or Google account.

1. On-device processing

Every tool in Astral — merging or splitting PDFs, converting or editing images, formatting JSON, transforming text — runs locally on your iPhone. The contents of the files and text you process are never sent off your device, never uploaded to a server, and never seen by us.

2. Information we collect

  • Usage analytics: Google Firebase Analytics collects standard, anonymized app-usage signals (screen views, session length, device model, OS version) so we can improve the app. This is aggregated and is not linked to your identity.
  • Advertising (AdMob): Unless you have purchased Remove Ads, Google AdMob serves banner and interstitial ads. With your permission (the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt), AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you still get non-personalised ads.
  • Purchases: Remove Ads is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase (StoreKit) or Google Play Billing, depending on your device. Apple or Google handles payment; we never see your card or billing details.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Google Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device model, OS version, and the state of the App at the moment of a crash) so we can find and fix bugs. These reports contain no personal information.

3. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not upload, read, or store the contents of your PDFs, images, or text.
  • We do not ask for your real name, phone number, contacts, calendar, microphone, or precise location.
  • Photos you pick are read only to process them on-device; results are saved only where you choose to export them.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics): anonymized app-usage analytics and crash reports. Firebase Privacy.
  • Google AdMob: serves ads and, with your consent, may use advertising identifiers. Google Ads Privacy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: processes the optional Remove Ads purchase. Apple Privacy · Google Privacy.

5. Ad consent and tracking choices

In the EU, UK, and EEA, Astral shows a consent form before requesting personalized ads. On iOS, the App asks for permission to track via the IDFA on first launch, used to personalise ads. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" does not limit any feature — every tool keeps working, with non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time: on iOS, in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, in Settings > Privacy > Ads. You can also manage AdMob's use of your data directly through Google at myadcenter.google.com.

6. Children

Astral is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data deletion

Uninstalling the App removes all on-device data. To request deletion of any analytics or crash data associated with your device, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.
