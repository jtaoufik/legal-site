Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Swipe up, down, left, or right to slide all tiles. When two matching plants collide, they bloom into the next stage. The run ends when the board is full and no merges are possible.
Does the run ever end?
Reaching the highest plant tier doesn't end the game. Keep merging and accumulating score until the board stalls. The further you go, the more points each merge is worth.
I lost my progress after updating
Bloom saves your in-progress run and best score on your device, so reinstalling the app clears the local save. To keep your progress across devices and reinstalls, use the optional Sign in with Apple offered after a few games (iOS). Your leaderboard row lives on our server and stays until you delete your account in Settings or contact us.
How does the leaderboard work?
Tap the trophy (Ladder) tab in the bottom bar to see the top 50 scores worldwide. Your handle (e.g. "WildLotus3142") is generated randomly on first launch and you can change it in Settings. The handle and your best score are stored on our server (Google Firebase Firestore) and shown publicly on the World Ladder, so please do not use your real name.
What is the Daily Challenge?
Every day Bloom gives you one goal plant to grow. Reach it to complete the day and build your streak. Miss a day and the streak starts over.
What is Zen mode?
Zen mode is a calmer way to play, with a light bamboo theme. Turn it on in Settings.
Can I remove ads?
Yes. A one-time "Remove ads" in-app purchase is available in Settings. If you already bought it, tap "Restore purchases" in Settings on your new device or after a reinstall. Without it, ads keep Bloom free and updated.
Does Bloom collect my email or personal data?
Signing in is optional. If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple may share your email (or a private relay address) and name with us to back up your progress. We use them only to link your progress and never show them to other players. You can delete your account at any time from Settings, which erases your handle, scores and leaderboard row.
The game crashed / a tile is stuck
Please email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com with: your iOS version, iPhone/iPad model, and what you were doing right before the issue. A screenshot helps a lot.
How is my data used?
See the Privacy Policy for the full answer. Short version: game progress lives on your device, your leaderboard handle and score are stored with Google Firebase, Sign in with Apple is optional, and AdMob shows ads. We don't collect your location.
Where can I leave a review?
If Bloom brings you a moment of calm, an App Store review is the kindest thing you can do. It takes 30 seconds and genuinely helps the game find more players.
