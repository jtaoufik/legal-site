Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

FootPredict - AI Bet Analyst (the "App") publishes football match probabilities. It is published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

Quick summary: FootPredict creates an anonymous account the first time you open it. We store your settings (language, country, followed teams and competitions, alert preferences), your subscription status and, if you allow notifications, your push token. Sign in with Apple is optional. The App has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Crash reports go to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs. It never asks for your name, phone number or location, and it takes no bets.

1. What we collect

  • User ID (linked to you): a random identifier created on your device and the account ID our server gives it. Used for app functionality.
  • Apple account identifier (linked to you, only if you choose Sign in with Apple): the stable identifier Apple shares with us. We request no name and no email. Used to keep your favourites and plan on several devices.
  • Purchases (linked to you): the App Store transaction records of your FootPredict subscription or Day Pass (product, dates, renewal and refund status), sent by your device and by Apple's server notifications. Used to unlock the content you paid for. We never see your payment card.
  • Preferences (linked to you): language, country, time zone, followed teams and competitions, alert switches and quiet hours. Used for app functionality.
  • Push token (linked to you, only if you allow notifications): the Apple Push Notification service token of your device. Used to send the alerts you switched on.
  • Crash data (not linked to you): when the App crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives the device model, iOS version, App version, a random installation ID and the state of the App at the moment of the crash. Used only to find and fix bugs.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, browsing history, advertising identifiers or health data. The App contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK; Firebase Analytics is not included.

2. Where it is stored

Data is stored on our own server and database hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Helsinki, Finland (European Union). Push notifications go through Apple Push Notification service. Purchases are processed by Apple. Crash reports are processed by Google Firebase Crashlytics (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

3. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account in Settings > Delete account removes your account, preferences, push token and transaction records from our database immediately. Server backups roll over within 30 days.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data and object to its processing. Use Delete account in the App, or write to taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

5. Age

The App is for adults (18+). It asks you to confirm your age before use and is not directed at children.

6. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date.
