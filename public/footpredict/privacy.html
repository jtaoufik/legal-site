Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

FootPredict (the "App") publishes football match probabilities and statistics. It is published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have. The App takes no bets and links to no bookmaker.

Quick summary: you sign in with Apple or Google so your plan, favourites and free unlocks follow you. We store your settings, your subscription status and, if you allow notifications, your push token. Free users can watch rewarded video ads (Appodeal) to unlock a prediction; those ads may use your device's advertising identifier only if you allow tracking. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics, no advertising identifier) are off by default in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland and can be switched off anywhere in Settings. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. We never ask for your name, phone number or location.

1. What we collect

  • Account ID (linked to you): the random identifier our server gives your account. Used for app functionality and, if you allow usage statistics, analytics.
  • Apple or Google account identifier (linked to you): the stable identifier Apple or Google shares when you sign in. We request no name and no email and store none. For Sign in with Apple we also keep a token that lets us revoke the link when you delete your account.
  • Purchases (linked to you): the App Store transaction records of your FootPredict subscription (product, dates, renewal and refund status). Used to unlock what you paid for. We never see your payment card.
  • Preferences (linked to you): language, country, time zone, followed teams and competitions, alert switches and quiet hours.
  • Push token (linked to you, only if you allow notifications): used to send the alerts you switched on.
  • Usage statistics (linked to your account ID, only if enabled): screens opened and actions such as opening the paywall or unlocking a prediction, sent to Google Firebase Analytics without any advertising identifier. Used to improve the App. Switch it off in Settings > Share usage statistics.
  • Crash data (linked to your account ID): device model, iOS version, App version and the App state at the moment of a crash, sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics. Used only to fix bugs.
  • Advertising data (only when you open a rewarded ad): to show rewarded video ads, Appodeal and its ad partners receive device and ad-interaction data and, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, the device advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EU, EEA and UK a consent form (Google UMP, IAB TCF) asks your choice first; ads are not shown before you decide. Subscribers see no ads.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, browsing history or health data, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Who receives it

  • Our server and database, hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH in Helsinki, Finland (EU).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, push notifications (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • Google: Google Sign-In, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  • Appodeal and the ad networks it works with, for rewarded ads only (appodeal.com/privacy-policy).

3. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Settings > Delete account removes your account, preferences, push token and transaction records from our database immediately, revokes the Sign in with Apple link and disconnects Google Sign-In. Server backups roll over within 30 days. Analytics and crash data expire under Google's retention settings (at most 14 months).

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, export or delete your data and object to its processing, and withdraw consent at any time (Settings > Share usage statistics; the ad consent form can be reopened from the unlock screen; tracking can be changed in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking). Use Delete account in the App, or write to taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

5. Age

The App is for adults (18+). It asks you to confirm your age before use and is not directed at children.

6. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date.
