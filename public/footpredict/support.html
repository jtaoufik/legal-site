Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel? Settings > Manage subscription in the App, or your Apple ID subscriptions in iOS Settings.

I paid but the App is locked. Settings > Restore purchases.

Why no value signal for my league? Value signals switch on only for competitions where the model beat the market on at least two seasons it had never seen. Elsewhere you still get probabilities, marked "model vs market".

Does FootPredict take bets? No. It publishes probabilities only.

How do I delete my account? Settings > Delete account.
