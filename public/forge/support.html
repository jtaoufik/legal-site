FAQ

How do I count my reps?

Forge gives you two ways. "Count by tapping" is the default: tap once per rep. If you prefer, choose "Use camera" in a workout and Forge counts for you using Apple's on-device Vision framework. No video is ever recorded, uploaded, or stored.

Does Forge need the camera?

No. The camera is optional and is only used when you pick "Use camera" in a workout. If you never pick it, Forge never asks to use it. Frames stay in memory only and are discarded immediately. You can revoke camera access any time in iOS Settings, Forge, Camera.

The camera isn't detecting my reps. What now?

Prop your phone so the front camera sees your whole body: head, shoulders, elbows, wrists. The visibility pill at the top right tells you how clearly Forge can see you. Aim for at least 80%. Even lighting helps too. You can always switch to "Count by tapping" instead.

What permissions does Forge need?

Screen Time (Family Controls) is required: it is how Forge blocks the apps you choose until you earn time. Onboarding asks only for this. Notifications are optional and the camera is optional.

How does app blocking work?

Forge uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to shield the apps you pick. Do your reps in Forge to unlock them for the time you earned. Without Screen Time access, Forge cannot block apps.

How is the push-up cost calculated?

You set it yourself in Schedule, under the push-up formula. Forge shows the cost for the time you request before you start.

Why turn on notifications?

Notifications are optional. If you allow them, Forge can remind you when your earned time ends. Forge works without them, and you can change this any time in iOS Settings, Forge, Notifications.

Delete my data?

All Forge data lives on your device. Delete the app to remove everything. There's no cloud copy.
