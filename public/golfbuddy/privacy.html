Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-11

GolfBuddy (the "App") is a 3D mini-golf game with solo courses and online matches, published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy says what data the App collects, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

Quick summary: you sign in with Apple or Google. We keep your name, email and a user id, your display name, your match shots and scores, and your in-game wallet on our own server. Google Analytics for Firebase measures how the App is used (you can switch that off in Settings) and Firebase Crashlytics reports crashes. We do not use the advertising identifier, we do not track you across other companies' apps, and this version has no ads. Credit packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase: Apple takes the payment, we never see your card, and we keep which pack you bought and when so the credits reach your wallet. You can delete your account in the App under Profile > Delete account.

1. What we collect

This list matches the App Privacy labels on the App Store. All of it is linked to your account.

  • Name and Email Address: given to us by Sign in with Apple or Google, and kept by Firebase Authentication. If you hide your email with Apple, we receive Apple's private relay address. Used for app functionality (signing you in); we send no marketing email.
  • User ID: an identifier created by Firebase Authentication and used as your account key on our server. Used for app functionality.
  • Other User Content: your display name, and the text you send through Profile > Help and feedback or a player report (Customer Support). Used for app functionality.
  • Gameplay Content: your match shots and scores, ladder rank, trophies and achievements, friends list, settings, and the balance of your in-game wallet (virtual currency and items, no cash value). Used for app functionality, for fair play checks and for ladders.
  • Product Interaction (Usage Data): events such as screens viewed, levels and matches finished. Collected by Google Analytics for Firebase, and by an event log on our own server. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Share usage data. Used for analytics.
  • Device ID: the Firebase app instance id that Google Analytics for Firebase creates for this installation. It is not the advertising identifier. Used for analytics.
  • Crash Data, Performance Data and Other Diagnostic Data: crash logs, performance measurements (device model, OS version, App version) and reports of errors inside the game (the error message and the place in our code, with the last screens opened), sent to Firebase Crashlytics. Used so we can find and fix bugs. Turning off Settings > Share usage data stops these reports too.
  • Purchase History: the credit packs you buy through Apple's In-App Purchase (which pack, when, and Apple's transaction id), kept on our server with your account. Used for app functionality: adding the credits to your wallet and answering a support request. Apple handles the payment; your card and billing details never reach us.

Not collected: location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing or search history, payment or other financial information.

Tracking: the App does not track you. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), it does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt, it contains no advertising, and it does not combine your data with data from other companies' apps or websites.

2. Where it is stored

  • Our server: your profile, matches, wallet, ladder rows and friends are stored in a PostgreSQL database on a server we rent from Hetzner in Germany.
  • Google Firebase: Authentication stores your sign-in data; Analytics and Crashlytics store usage and crash data. Google LLC operates them and may process data in the United States.
  • On your device: settings and cached game data, removed when you uninstall the App.

3. Third parties

We share data only with the services below, for the purposes stated. We do not sell your data.

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics): see Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and the App Store and TestFlight that distribute the App. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Hetzner: hosts our server.
  • Resend: sends the occasional service email (replies to your support requests); we send no marketing email.

4. Your choices

  • Usage data: switch off Settings > Share usage data. Collection stops for Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Display name: you can change it in your Profile; names are filtered on our server.
  • Sign-in: required, because ladders, friends, duels and the economy run on our server and need one identity per player.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. To delete it, open Profile > Delete account in the App and confirm. We then delete your profile, wallet, friends and ladder rows from our server, revoke the Sign in with Apple token, delete your Firebase Authentication account and reset your Analytics data. Past match records are kept only in anonymised form, shown as "Deleted player", so other players' match histories stay correct. Analytics and crash data held by Google follow Google's retention settings for our Firebase project. You can also ask us to delete data by email (see Contact).

6. Children

GolfBuddy is rated 4+ and is not in the Kids category. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Other players and house players

Other players see your display name and match results in ladders and matches. Matches may include computer-controlled players.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top will change when we do.
