FAQ

How do I sign in?

GolfBuddy uses Sign in with Apple or Google on the first screen. Any Apple ID or Google account works. If sign-in fails, check your connection and try again; if it keeps failing, contact us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile > Delete account and confirm. Your profile, wallet, friends and ladder rows are deleted; past matches stay only as "Deleted player". See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a player?

Tap the report option on the player's row in Ranks, or on the match result. We review reports within 72 hours. To stop meeting a player, block them from Friends.

Who will I play against?

Matches may include computer-controlled players. We use them so you always find a game; they play at a level close to yours.

How do I contact you or ask for my data to be removed?

Email goloio32@gmail.com or use Profile > Contact us. Include your display name so we can find your account.
