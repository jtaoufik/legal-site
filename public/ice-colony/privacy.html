Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Ice Colony (the "App") is a penguin and ice puzzle game published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy says what data the App collects, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

Quick summary: you can play without an account: the App signs you in silently with an anonymous Firebase identifier. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and only backs up your progress to that account. Your progress is saved on your device and, optionally, backed up on our own server in the European Union. That server also holds your player name, level and country for the leaderboards and teams. There is no chat: the only text other players can see is your player name and your team name. Google Firebase measures how the App is used (Analytics) only if you allow it, reports crashes (Crashlytics) and delivers push notifications (Cloud Messaging). Purchases go through Apple: we never see your card. Full-screen and rewarded adverts from Google AdMob are shown with your consent where the law requires it. You can erase everything in the App under Settings > Delete my data, or by email.

1. What we collect

  • Anonymous identifier: at first launch the App signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random user id that is not linked to your name, email or phone. We use it as the key of your cloud save and of everything we store about you on our server (see the next item). Firebase also creates an installation id for the App on your device.
  • Optional Sign in with Apple or Google: after a win, and in Settings > My progress, the App offers to sign in with Apple or Google so your progress can be restored on a new device. It is never required: "Not now" keeps you playing as a guest. If you sign in, Firebase Authentication links your anonymous identifier to that Apple or Google account and stores the account identifier and the email address it provides (with Apple you can hide your real email; Apple may share your name the first time). We use this only to recognise you when you sign in again. It is not used for ads, analytics or marketing, and we do not send you email.
  • Leaderboards and teams (linked to your anonymous id on our server): your player name (3 to 16 characters, chosen by you or generated), your current level, the App Store storefront country (two letters, used for the country ranking), your team membership and team name, the life requests and gifts you exchange with teammates, and the reports you file about a player name or a team name. Leaderboards show other players your name, your level and your country ranking. Teammates see your name and level. Everyone can see a team's name, badge and score.
  • Player and team names: there is no free-text chat in the App. The only text you write that other players can see is your player name and your team name. Both are filtered for offensive words, and any player can report a name or a team name from inside the App.
  • Gameplay data (Gameplay Content): your level reached, coins, boosters, achievements, your Collection cards, settings and the list of your purchases and spends in the game. It is stored on your device. Collection cards stay in your save: nothing extra is sent to our server for them. If the cloud save is available, the same data is stored on our server under your anonymous user id so that progress survives a reinstall of the App on the same phone (a new phone starts with a new anonymous identifier, so its progress starts fresh).
  • Usage data (Product Interaction): Google Analytics for Firebase events such as level started, level ended (won or lost, time, moves, boosters used), tutorial completed, screen viewed, team joined (team number only), booster used and shop opened, plus your level range (for example "11-50"), with technical context such as App version and OS version. Used to balance the game and fix problems. Analytics is off until the consent choice in the App allows it, and it stays off if you refuse.
  • Crash data and diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics receives crash logs and performance measurements (device model, OS version, App version, state of the App at the time of the crash).
  • Performance data: Firebase Performance Monitoring measures how fast the App starts, how screens render and how long network requests to our server take, with device model, OS version and App version. It is not linked to your identifier. Like Analytics, it is off until the consent choice in the App allows it, and it stays off if you refuse. With that consent, crash reports also carry your anonymous identifier so we can follow a crash you report; without it they stay unlinked.
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token so we can send reminders and event messages. You can refuse or turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Purchase history: what you bought (for example the "no ads" upgrade or coin packs), when, and Apple's transaction id. Apple handles the payment. Your card and billing details never reach us.
  • Advertising data: the App shows full-screen adverts between levels and rewarded adverts that you choose to watch, served by Google AdMob. There are no banner adverts. AdMob may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, plus technical data such as IP address and device type. If you buy the "no ads" upgrade, adverts are removed.

Not collected: unless you choose to sign in with Apple or Google (see above), your real name and email address; and in every case your phone number, GPS or other device location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history. The only location-like data is the App Store storefront country described above (two letters, for the country ranking), which we declare to Apple as coarse location.

2. Advertising, consent and tracking

The App uses Google AdMob, and it asks for your choice before showing personalised adverts. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown first. Before any identifier is used for advertising, iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency question. If you decline, AdMob does not receive the advertising identifier and you see non-personalised adverts; nothing else in the App changes. You can change the iOS answer in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

3. Where it is stored

  • Our server: the optional cloud save, your anonymous player row, name, level, country, team data, requests, gifts and reports are stored in a PostgreSQL database on a server we rent from Hetzner in the European Union.
  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring and Cloud Messaging are operated by Google LLC, which may process data in the United States.
  • On your device: your local save, settings and caches. They are removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Third parties

We share data only with the services below, for the purposes stated. We do not sell your data.

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging): see Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Google AdMob: serves the full-screen and rewarded adverts. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Apple: the App Store, in-app purchases and push delivery. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Hetzner: hosts our server.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your cloud save, player row and team data until you delete your data. We do not run an automatic clean-up of inactive anonymous accounts. To delete everything, open Settings > Delete my data in the App and confirm. The App then asks our server to delete your player row with its scores, team membership, life requests, gifts and the reports you made, your cloud save, deletes your Firebase anonymous user and installation id, removes your push token, cancels scheduled notifications and erases the save and caches on your device, then starts again from level 1 with a new anonymous identifier. If the server cannot be reached, the data on your device is still erased at once and the server deletion is retried at the next launch. Purchases already made cannot be refunded by deleting your data; refunds are handled by Apple. The "no ads" upgrade belongs to your Apple account, so it can be restored with Restore Purchases.

You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact). If you did not sign in, the identifier is anonymous, so the in-app button is the most reliable way. Delete my data also removes your sign-in link and the email stored with it. If you cannot use it, tell us your device model and approximate dates and we will do what we can. Analytics and crash data held by Google follow Google's retention settings for our Firebase project.

6. Children

Ice Colony is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of the data linked to you, object to its processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address below.
