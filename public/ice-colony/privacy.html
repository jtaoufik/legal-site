Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-15

Ice Colony (the "App") is a penguin and ice puzzle game published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy says what data the App collects, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

Quick summary: there is no account and no login. The App creates an anonymous identifier for your device. Your progress is saved on your device and, optionally, backed up on our own server in the European Union. Google Firebase measures how the App is used (Analytics) and reports crashes (Crashlytics), and delivers push notifications (Cloud Messaging). Purchases go through Apple: we never see your card. Adverts from Google AdMob are shown only in versions that include them, with your consent where the law requires it. You can erase everything in the App under Settings > Delete my data, or by email.

1. What we collect

  • Anonymous identifier: at first launch the App signs you in anonymously with Firebase Authentication. This creates a random user id that is not linked to your name, email or phone. We use it as the key of your cloud save and your leaderboard row. Firebase also creates an installation id for the App on your device.
  • Gameplay data (Gameplay Content): your level reached, coins, boosters, achievements, settings and the list of your purchases and spends in the game. It is stored on your device. If the cloud save is available, the same data is stored on our server under your anonymous user id so that progress survives a reinstall or a new phone.
  • Usage data (Product Interaction): Google Analytics for Firebase events such as level started, level won, level failed, booster used and shop opened, with technical context such as App version and OS version. Used to balance the game and fix problems.
  • Crash data and diagnostics: Firebase Crashlytics receives crash logs and performance measurements (device model, OS version, App version, state of the App at the time of the crash).
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token so we can send reminders and event messages. You can refuse or turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Purchase history: what you bought (for example the "no ads" upgrade or coin packs), when, and Apple's transaction id. Apple handles the payment. Your card and billing details never reach us.
  • Advertising data: versions of the App that show adverts use Google AdMob. AdMob may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, plus technical data such as IP address and device type. If you buy the "no ads" upgrade, adverts are removed.

Not collected: name, email address, phone number, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, health data, browsing history.

2. Advertising, consent and tracking

Where the App includes Google AdMob, it asks for your choice before showing personalised adverts. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form is shown first. Before any identifier is used for advertising, iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency question. If you decline, AdMob does not receive the advertising identifier and you see non-personalised adverts; nothing else in the App changes. You can change the iOS answer in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

3. Where it is stored

  • Our server: the optional cloud save, your anonymous player row and your leaderboard progress are stored in a PostgreSQL database on a server we rent from Hetzner in the European Union.
  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging are operated by Google LLC, which may process data in the United States.
  • On your device: your local save, settings and caches. They are removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Third parties

We share data only with the services below, for the purposes stated. We do not sell your data.

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging): see Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Google AdMob: serves adverts in versions that include them. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Apple: the App Store, in-app purchases and push delivery. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Hetzner: hosts our server.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your cloud save while your anonymous user exists. To delete everything, open Settings > Delete my data in the App and confirm. The App then asks our server to delete your player row, cloud save and leaderboard entries, deletes your Firebase anonymous user and installation id, removes your push token, cancels scheduled notifications and erases the save and caches on your device, then starts again from level 1 with a new anonymous identifier. If the server cannot be reached, the data on your device is still erased at once and the server deletion is retried at the next launch. Purchases already made cannot be refunded by deleting your data; refunds are handled by Apple. The "no ads" upgrade belongs to your Apple account, so it can be restored with Restore Purchases.

You can also ask us to delete your data by email (see Contact). Because the identifier is anonymous, the in-app button is the most reliable way. If you cannot use it, tell us your device model and approximate dates and we will do what we can. Analytics and crash data held by Google follow Google's retention settings for our Firebase project.

6. Children

Ice Colony is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of the data linked to you, object to its processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address below.
