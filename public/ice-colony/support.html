FAQ

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. When the cloud save is available it is also backed up anonymously on our server, so it can follow you to a new phone. There is no account to create.

I bought "no ads" or coins on a new phone and they are gone.

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. The "no ads" upgrade belongs to your Apple account and comes back.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings, tap Delete my data and confirm. This erases your progress, coins and purchase history on your device and on our servers, and starts the game again at level 1. Purchases already made cannot be refunded here. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I stop notifications or tracking?

Use the iOS Settings app: Notifications > Ice Colony, and Privacy & Security > Tracking.
