Delete your account

Last updated September 8, 2026

Introva is published by Taoufik Jabbari. You can delete your Introva account and everything attached to it from inside the app, on iPhone and on Android, without contacting us.

In the app: open Settings, scroll to the bottom, tap Delete my account and confirm. The deletion runs immediately.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (the Apple or Google identity linked to Introva) and your account record.
  • Every journal entry, mood and voice transcription.
  • Every weekly report and the profile built from your entries.
  • Your reminder settings, your notification token and your place on the waitlist if you had one.

Everything above is removed from our server within 24 hours. Encrypted backups of the server are rotated within 30 days, after which no copy remains.

What is kept

  • The purchase record of the App itself, held by Apple or Google. It belongs to your App Store or Google Play account and is governed by their terms; we hold nothing about it.
  • Anonymous crash and performance data already collected. It is not linked to your journal and expires on its own.

Delete only some data

You do not have to delete the whole account to remove content. Any entry can be deleted from the journal by opening it and choosing Delete; the weekly report is rebuilt without it. You can also export all your entries and reports from Settings before deleting anything.

If you can no longer open the app

Email goloio32@gmail.com from the address you use with Apple or Google, with the subject "Introva account deletion". We confirm the request with a reply and delete the account within 7 days. No other information is needed.

Details on the data Introva handles are in the Privacy Policy.
