Privacy Policy

Last updated September 5, 2026

Introva (the "App") is a private journaling app published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). Introva lets you log your day by text or voice and turns your entries into a weekly report. This policy explains what data the App handles, who we share it with, and your choices.

Quick summary: Your journal entries and moods are encrypted at rest on our server in Germany. Once a week they are analysed to generate your report. We sign you in with your email or your Apple relay address through Firebase Auth, and use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.

1. Information the App handles

  • Account identity: your email address, or the private relay address Apple gives us if you sign in with "Hide My Email," through Firebase Auth. We use this only to identify your account and sign you in.
  • Journal entries and moods: the text or transcribed voice entries you write, and the mood you attach to them. They are encrypted at rest on our server, hosted in Germany.
  • Weekly report: the summary generated from your entries once a week, stored on your account so you can read it back.
  • Subscription state: whether you have an active subscription, reported to us by Apple. We do not see your payment details.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics records standard, anonymized usage events (screens opened, sessions started) so we can understand how the App is used.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device model, OS version, and the state of the App at the moment of a crash) so we can find and fix bugs.

2. How the AI report is made

Once a week, Introva analyses your entries to write your report. This analysis is performed by our AI processing provider under contract; your entries are not used to train any model and are removed from the processing environment after each run.

3. Your data, your control

You can download all your data or delete it at any time from the app's Settings, without contacting us. Deleting your data removes every entry, report and profile from our systems within 24 hours; deleting your account also removes your sign-in. We never sell your data and we do not show ads.

4. What we do not collect

  • We do not access your microphone beyond transcribing a voice entry you choose to record, and we do not keep the audio itself.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts.
  • We do not collect any health data (no HealthKit, no fitness or medical records).
  • We do not show ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete individual entries at any time in the App. You can export your entries and reports from the App before deleting them. Deleting your account deletes your entries, moods, reports, and the AI profile file from our server. Analytics and crash data are retained only as long as needed to operate and improve the App and are not tied to your journal content.

6. Not therapy

Introva is a journaling and self-reflection tool. It is not therapy, not a medical device, and not a substitute for professional mental health care. If you are in crisis or need medical advice, please contact a qualified professional or your local emergency services.
