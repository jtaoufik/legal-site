FAQ

What is the waitlist, and why is there a cap?

Introva accepts up to 10,000 accounts so the weekly AI report can run reliably on the infrastructure we operate. Once the cap is reached, new sign-ups join an email waitlist and are let in as capacity frees up. You will get an email as soon as your spot opens.

When do I get my weekly report?

Your entries for the week are processed once a week and your report is ready shortly after. You can read every past report in the App at any time.

How do I get my data out?

In the App, go to Settings, Download my data.

How do I delete my data?

In the App, go to Settings, Delete my data, or Settings, Delete my account. Both ask you to confirm your identity first.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Cancel any time in your device Settings under your Apple ID, Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; your current billing period stays active until it ends.

Is Introva a substitute for therapy?

No. Introva is a journaling and self-reflection tool, not therapy or medical advice. See our Terms for details.
