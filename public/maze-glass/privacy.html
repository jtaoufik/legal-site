Privacy Policy

Last updated September 4, 2026

Maze Glass (the "App") is a single-player maze game published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, who we share it with, and your choices.

Quick summary: Maze Glass stores your progress on your device. It uses Google Firebase to anonymously record your scores on a global leaderboard, to gather aggregate usage analytics, and to report crashes. Google AdMob shows ads (with your consent choice in the EU/UK and your App Tracking Transparency choice on iOS), and a one-time Remove Ads purchase is available.

1. Information we collect

  • Game progress (on your device): your current quest level, best times, and best scores per maze. Stored locally and removed when you uninstall.
  • Leaderboard data (anonymous): an anonymous user ID generated on first launch. Together with a randomly-generated display name (e.g. "GlassCipher472"), your country code (derived from your device's region setting), and your scores, this ID is stored in Google Firestore so the global leaderboard can rank you. The display name is auto-generated and can be edited; no real personal info is collected.
  • Aggregate usage analytics: Firebase Analytics collects standard, anonymized usage signals (screen views, session length, OS version). This data is aggregated and is not linked to you personally.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device model, OS version, and the state of the App at the moment of a crash) so we can find and fix bugs. These reports contain no personal information.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob shows banner and interstitial ads. On iOS, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show personalized ads only if you allow tracking through the App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you decline, AdMob shows only non-personalized ads. In the EU/UK/EEA, a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before any personalized ad is requested.
  • Purchases: Maze Glass offers an optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase, processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit) or by Google through Google Play Billing. Apple or Google handles the payment; we never receive your card number or payment details.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, or photos.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, camera, or precise location.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties.

3. How third parties use data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics): anonymous identity, leaderboard storage, usage analytics, and crash reporting. See Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Google AdMob: serves banner and interstitial ads and may collect advertising identifiers, subject to your consent and tracking choices below. See Google's Ads Privacy and AdMob data practices.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: processes the optional Remove Ads purchase. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

4. Ad consent and tracking choices

In the EU, UK, and EEA, Maze Glass shows a consent form before requesting personalized ads. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt governs whether AdMob may use your advertising identifier:

  • Allow: AdMob may serve personalized ads based on your activity in other apps.
  • Ask App Not To Track: AdMob serves only non-personalized ads. The game stays fully playable either way.

You can change your choice at any time: on iOS, in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; on Android, in Settings > Privacy > Ads. You can also manage AdMob's use of your data directly through Google at myadcenter.google.com. Purchasing Remove Ads removes all ads.

5. Children's privacy

Maze Glass is rated 4+ and suitable for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided any identifiable information, please email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

On-device data is removed when you uninstall the App. To delete your leaderboard entry, account, and everything tied to your anonymous ID, open Settings inside the App and use "Delete my data" — this removes your Firestore leaderboard row and Firebase account and clears local data immediately. You can also request deletion by emailing taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com with the display name shown in your Settings tab.

7. International transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google LLC (USA). Data sent to those services may be stored or processed in the United States. By using Maze Glass you consent to such transfer where applicable.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in an updated App Store / Play Store release.
