Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

Nightfall Squad (the "App") is a game published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App uses, why, who receives it and the choices you have.

Quick summary: there is no account and no sign-in in this version. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The App shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Appodeal (with Google AdMob inside); the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking, and in the EEA and UK a Google consent form asks first. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics. In multiplayer you may turn on voice chat, which is streamed live and never recorded. The App is not directed at children under 13.

1. What the App uses

  • Game data on your device: progress, settings, coins, hearts and unlocked items. This stays on your device and we do not receive it.
  • Advertising data: to show rewarded and interstitial ads, Appodeal and its ad partners, including Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), receive device and ad-interaction data and, only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt (App Tracking Transparency), the device advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EEA and UK a consent form (Google UMP) asks your choice first. Personalised ads are shown only with your consent; otherwise ads are not personalised.
  • Crash data: device model, iOS version, App version and the App state at the time of a crash, sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics. It is diagnostic data and is not linked to your identity. Used only to fix bugs.
  • Multiplayer data: when you play with other people, the game exchanges the match information needed to play (position, actions, a display name for the match) through our match service.
  • Voice chat (optional): multiplayer supports in-game voice chat for up to 4 players, provided by LiveKit. The microphone is muted by default and iOS asks for microphone permission only when you turn the mic on. Audio is streamed live to the other players in your match. It is never recorded or stored by us.
  • Purchases: the optional "Remove ads" subscription is handled by Apple. We see the subscription status needed to remove ads, never your payment card.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or health data, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Who receives it

  • Appodeal and the ad networks it works with, including Google AdMob, for ads (appodeal.com/privacy-policy, policies.google.com/privacy).
  • Google: Firebase Crashlytics (firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
  • LiveKit, the real-time audio provider, only if you use voice chat (livekit.io/legal/privacy-policy).
  • Apple: App Store purchases and subscription management (apple.com/legal/privacy).

3. Your choices

  • Tracking: change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Ad consent in the EEA and UK: you can change your choice through the consent form in the App settings.
  • Microphone: keep the mic muted, or switch it off in iOS Settings > Nightfall Squad.
  • Delete your local data by deleting the App.

Under the GDPR and similar laws you can access, correct or delete personal data we hold and object to its processing. Write to taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

5. Changes

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date.
