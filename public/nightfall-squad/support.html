Frequently asked questions

How do I remove ads? Subscribe to Remove ads in the App. Rewarded ads stay available as an option.

How do I cancel the subscription? In iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Nightfall Squad. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I paid but ads still show. Use Restore purchases in the App.

How do I mute or report a player? In a multiplayer match, open the player list, then tap mute or report next to the player. We act on reports within 24 hours.

Is voice chat recorded? No. It is live only, muted by default, and the microphone is requested only when you turn it on.

Do I need an account? No. Your progress is stored on your device.
