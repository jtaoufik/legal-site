Apps · Nine: Sudoku Puzzle

Nine

A sudoku you can trust.

Every grid in Nine is graded by the technique it actually demands, not by how many squares are blank. When Nine says Expert, you will need an expert's move. Five levels, from Easy to Evil.

One sudoku a day, the same for everyone

Every player in the world gets the identical daily grid, so times are comparable and the world ladder means something. It resets at midnight UTC. Keep the streak going.

Hints that teach

Stuck? A hint fills the next square you could have deduced, and names the technique that proves it: naked single, hidden pair, pointing pair, X-Wing. Most sudoku apps just give you the number. Each hint adds 30 seconds to the time that counts, shown openly on the win screen, so the ladder stays fair.

Everything a sudoku player expects

  • Pencil marks, with candidates
  • Unlimited undo
  • Duplicate highlighting you can turn off
  • A timer you can hide
  • Best times and win rate for every difficulty
  • Auto-save: close the app mid-grid and come back to it
  • Export your progress and carry it to a new phone

Eight languages, following your device automatically. Free, with ads.
