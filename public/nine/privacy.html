Apps · Nine: Sudoku Puzzle · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

Nine: Sudoku Puzzle (the "App") is a sudoku puzzle game published by Chronos Studio .Inc ("we", "us"), available on the App Store (bundle com.taoufikjabbari.ninesudoku) and Google Play (package com.taoufikjabbari.nine). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Quick summary: Nine keeps your puzzles, records, and settings on your device only. If you choose to complete the optional daily puzzle, we send a display name, country code, and completion time to Google Firebase under a random device ID, not an account. Google AdMob shows ads, and Firebase Analytics records anonymized usage. We do not collect your real name, email, phone, or precise location, and no account or sign-in is required to play.

1. Information we collect

Nine collects the minimum information needed to deliver the game's features:

  • Game progress (on your device only): your records, best times, daily streak, the puzzle currently in progress, and your chosen language. Stored in local storage on your device. This never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.
  • World leaderboard (optional, anonymous): if you complete the daily puzzle, the App sends a display name (chosen by you in Settings — the default is simply "Player", 16 characters max), a two-letter country code derived from your device's region setting, and your completion time in seconds to Google Firebase Firestore. This is keyed to a random identifier generated on your device — not to any account, email, or advertising ID.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics records standard app usage events — puzzle started, puzzle won (with its difficulty and duration), hints used, and language chosen — so we can understand how the game is played. We do not build an advertising profile from this data.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) to show ads, subject to the consent and tracking choices described in section 4.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: standard App Store / Play Store crash reports may be shared with us, anonymized and aggregated.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, sign-in, email address, or name to play.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not collect precise or IP-based location — only the two-letter country code derived from your device's region setting, used for the leaderboard.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties.

3. How third parties use data

Nine uses two third-party services, both operated by Google:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, project "nine-sudoku-app", + Analytics): stores the optional world leaderboard entries and reports anonymized usage events. See Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Google AdMob: serves ads within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping. See Google's policy and AdMob data practices.

4. Ad consent and tracking choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, a Google-certified consent message (UMP) is shown before any personalized ads are served, letting you choose whether to allow personalization. You can reopen these privacy choices at any time from within the App's Settings.

On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt governs whether AdMob may use your advertising identifier:

  • Allow: AdMob may serve personalized ads based on your activity in other apps.
  • Ask App Not To Track: AdMob serves only non-personalized ads. The game stays fully playable either way.

You can change this any time in your device's system settings (iOS: Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: ad settings under Google in system settings).

5. Exporting and importing your progress

You can export your records, streak, and puzzle progress to a JSON file from within the App, and import that file on another device to carry your progress across. This file is created and shared entirely at your own initiative — it never leaves your device unless you choose to send or transfer it yourself, and we never see or receive a copy of it.

6. Children's privacy

Nine is suitable for all audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has submitted a leaderboard entry, email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com and we will remove it.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Because Nine has no account system, there is no profile to access or export beyond what is already on your device. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it permanently removes everything held locally, including your records, streak, and puzzle in progress. To have a leaderboard entry removed, email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com with the display name and approximate date shown next to your entry, and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google LLC (USA). Data sent to those services may be stored or processed in the United States. By using Nine you consent to such transfer where applicable.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. The "Effective" date above will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in an updated App Store / Play Store release.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com.
