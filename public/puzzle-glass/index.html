Puzzle Glass

A relaxing jigsaw, one artwork at a time.

Puzzle Glass is a jigsaw puzzle game built around ten hand-crafted artworks, each cut into 30 to 48 pieces. Puzzles unlock one after another: finish one, and the next opens. No energy meters, no timers pressuring you — just the picture, the pieces, and however long you want to take.

A board that gets out of your way

Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and place pieces exactly where they belong. From the third puzzle onward, pieces can arrive rotated — tap a piece to turn it until it sits right. A ghost hint overlays a faint image of the finished artwork on the board whenever you want a nudge, and you can switch it off just as easily.

Track it, or don't

A timer and a move counter run for every puzzle, so you can chase a cleaner solve if that is your thing. If it is not, ignore them — nothing is ranked and nothing is shared. All your progress is stored locally on your device, and nowhere else.

What's inside

  • 10 hand-crafted artworks, unlocked sequentially
  • 30 to 48 pieces per puzzle
  • Piece rotation from puzzle 3 onward
  • Zoom and pan on the board
  • Ghost hint you can toggle any time
  • Timer and move counter per puzzle
  • Auto-save: leave mid-puzzle and come back to it

Free, with ads. iOS only, on the App Store.
