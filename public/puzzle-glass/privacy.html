Apps · Puzzle Glass: Jigsaw Art · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 28, 2026

Puzzle Glass: Jigsaw Art (the "App") is a jigsaw puzzle game published by Chronos Studio .Inc ("we", "us"), available on the App Store (bundle com.taoufikjabbari.puzzleglass). This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Quick summary: Puzzle Glass keeps your puzzle progress, times, and settings on your device only — game progress never leaves your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no leaderboard, and no cloud sync. Google AdMob shows ads, Firebase Analytics records anonymized usage, and Firebase Crashlytics reports crashes. We do not collect your real name, email, phone, or precise location.

1. Information we collect

Puzzle Glass collects the minimum information needed to deliver the game's features:

  • Game progress (on your device only): which puzzles you have unlocked and completed, the puzzle currently in progress, your best times and move counts, and your settings (such as the ghost hint toggle). Stored in local storage on your device. This never leaves your device.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics records standard, anonymized app usage events — puzzle started, puzzle completed (with its duration and move count), hint toggled — so we can understand how the game is played. We do not build an advertising profile from this data.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show banner and interstitial ads, subject to the consent and tracking choices described in section 4.
  • Crash and diagnostic data: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports (device model, iOS version, and the state of the app at the moment of a crash) so we can find and fix bugs. These reports contain no personal information.

2. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not require an account, sign-in, email address, or name to play.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera.
  • We do not collect precise or IP-based location.
  • There is no leaderboard, no cloud save, and no sync — your game progress is never uploaded anywhere.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to third parties.

3. How third parties use data

Puzzle Glass uses two third-party services, both operated by Google:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics): reports anonymized usage events and crash reports. See Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Google AdMob: serves banner and interstitial ads within the App. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and frequency capping. See Google's policy and AdMob data practices.

4. Ad consent and tracking choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, a Google-certified consent message (UMP) is shown before any personalized ads are served, letting you choose whether to allow personalization. You can reopen these privacy choices at any time from within the App's Settings.

On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt governs whether AdMob may use your advertising identifier:

  • Allow: AdMob may serve personalized ads based on your activity in other apps.
  • Ask App Not To Track: AdMob serves only non-personalized ads. The game stays fully playable either way.

You can change this any time in your device's system settings (Privacy & Security → Tracking).

5. Children's privacy

Puzzle Glass is suitable for all audiences but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com and we will remove it.

6. Your rights and data deletion

Because Puzzle Glass has no account system, there is no profile to access or export beyond what is already on your device. Deleting the App permanently removes everything held locally, including your unlocked puzzles, times, and the puzzle in progress. For any other deletion request — for example, analytics or crash data tied to your device — email taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com and we will handle it.

7. International transfers

Firebase and AdMob are operated by Google LLC (USA). Data sent to those services may be stored or processed in the United States. By using Puzzle Glass you consent to such transfer where applicable.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy. The "Effective" date above will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in an updated App Store release.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact taoufik.leon.jabbari@gmail.com.
