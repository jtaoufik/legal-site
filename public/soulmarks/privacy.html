Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

Soulmarks ("the App") lets people leave short messages on the ground of real places and read the ones others left, through the camera. It is published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name or e-mail, we do not record or upload camera video, and we do not track you across apps or sell data. Your location is used while the app is open.

1. What the App handles

  • Sign in with Apple. Posting and rating need it. We request no name and no e-mail. Firebase Authentication stores an account identifier tied to your Apple sign-in. Reading marks needs only an anonymous Firebase account.
  • Location. Used while the app is open, to find marks near you and to attach a mark you leave to its spot. A mark stores the coordinates of the spot where it lies. Your movements are not stored.
  • Camera. Used live to show marks in your surroundings. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • Marks. The phrase and words you choose (never free text), the position and direction, a timestamp, and the appraisals, poor ratings and reports it receives. Marks are visible to other readers near them, linked to an anonymous identifier, not to your name.
  • Ratings and reports you give, so that nobody can rate or report a mark twice.
  • Purchases. The optional "Extra mark" purchase is handled by Apple. We record the transaction identifier to grant the mark once.

2. Where it is stored

Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore, region Europe). We use no advertising and no third-party analytics SDK.

3. Fading and retention

Marks fade and are removed automatically: after about 30 days, longer when appraised, sooner when rated poor or reported.

4. Your choices

  • Delete your account and all your marks in the app: Settings, Delete account.
  • Erase a single mark yourself by standing at it and holding to erase.
  • Report any mark from its reading screen.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.
