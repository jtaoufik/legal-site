Soulmarks

How do I leave a mark?

Open the app, point the camera at the floor, tap Leave a mark, choose a phrase and a word, and confirm. Sign in with Apple is needed to leave marks; reading is free.

Why can't I leave a sixth mark?

Five marks are free. To leave another, erase one of yours by standing at it and holding to erase, or buy an Extra mark in the app.

I bought an Extra mark but it did not appear

Keep the app open for a moment. If it still does not show, contact us with your Apple receipt.

Report a mark

Open a mark and tap Report. Reported marks fade after a few reports.
