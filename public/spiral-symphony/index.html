Spiral Symphony

A marble music game for iPhone: slide your finger to turn a spiral tower of piano keys, and every landing of the glass marble plays the next note of the song. 74 public-domain classical pieces and Christmas carols. Play without an account, or sign in with Apple to keep your progress on every device.
