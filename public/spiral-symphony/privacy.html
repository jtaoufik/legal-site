Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Spiral Symphony (the "App") is a marble and music game published by Taoufik Jabbari ("we", "us"). This policy says what data the App collects, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it.

Quick summary: you can play without an account. Signing in with Apple is optional and only backs up your progress so you can find it on another device. Google Firebase measures how the App is used (Analytics, which you can turn off in the App), reports crashes (Crashlytics), measures performance and delivers notifications (Cloud Messaging). Purchases go through Apple: we never see your card. Full-screen and rewarded adverts from Appodeal or Google AdMob are shown with your consent where the law requires it. You can delete your account and its data in the App.

1. What we collect

  • Account (optional): if you sign in with Apple, Firebase Authentication receives a user identifier, and the name and email address Apple shares (the email can be an Apple private relay address). If you never sign in, no account is created.
  • Progress (optional cloud save): when you are signed in, your stars, scores, coins, hearts, marble skins and favourites are saved with Google Cloud Firestore under your user identifier, so they come back on another device. Without sign-in, progress stays on your device only.
  • Usage data: Firebase Analytics records events such as a song started or finished, screens opened and purchases started or completed, with your user identifier when you are signed in. It does not use your device's advertising identifier. You can turn it off in Profile > Account > Share anonymous usage data.
  • Crash and performance data: Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring receive crash reports and timings with the device model, iOS version and App version. They are not linked to your identity.
  • Push token: if you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a device token so we can remind you, for example, when your hearts are full. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Purchase history: what you bought (for example coin packs or the "no ads" upgrade) and when. Apple handles the payment; your card and billing details never reach us.
  • Advertising data: the App shows full-screen adverts between songs and rewarded adverts that you choose to watch, served by Appodeal (a mediation service that runs an auction between advertising networks) or by Google AdMob. There are no banner adverts. The advertising provider and the networks taking part may receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, plus technical data such as IP address (from which they may infer an approximate location) and device type. If you buy the "no ads" upgrade, full-screen adverts are removed; rewarded adverts stay available only when you choose to watch one.

Not collected: your phone number, GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or browsing history.

2. Advertising, consent and tracking

The App uses Appodeal or Google AdMob (one at a time), and it asks for your choice before showing personalised adverts. Where the law requires consent before adverts (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), this version shows no adverts at all; a consent form will be added in a later version before adverts are shown there. Before any identifier is used for advertising, iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency question. If you decline, no advertising provider receives the advertising identifier and you see non-personalised adverts; nothing else in the App changes. You can change the iOS answer in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

3. Where it is stored

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Remote Config and Cloud Messaging are operated by Google LLC, which may process data in the United States.
  • On your device: your local progress, settings and downloaded songs. They are removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Third parties

We share data only with the services below, for the purposes stated. We do not sell your data.

  • Google (Firebase): see Firebase's privacy notice.
  • Appodeal: serves the full-screen and rewarded adverts through its mediation, with networks such as AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Liftoff (Vungle), ironSource, InMobi, BidMachine, BIGO Ads and Digital Turbine (DT Exchange). See the Appodeal privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob: serves the full-screen and rewarded adverts when the App uses it instead of Appodeal. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, the App Store, in-app purchases and push delivery. See Apple's privacy policy.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account and cloud save until you delete them. To delete them, open Profile > Account > Delete account in the App and confirm: the App deletes your cloud save, revokes the Sign in with Apple link, deletes your Firebase user and erases the progress on your iPhone. Purchases belong to your Apple account and can be restored with Restore Purchases; refunds are handled by Apple. You can also ask us by email (see Contact). Analytics and crash data held by Google follow Google's retention settings for our Firebase project.

6. Children

Spiral Symphony is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of the data linked to you, object to its processing, and complain to your data protection authority. Contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the date at the top will change when we do.
