Support

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Shop, or the Profile tab (the fifth tab of the bottom bar), and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple account used to buy.

I lost my progress.

Without an account, progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the App or changing phone starts a new game. Sign in with Apple in Profile > Account to back up your progress and find it on another device. Purchases come back with Restore purchases either way.
